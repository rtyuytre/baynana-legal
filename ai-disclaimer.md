# AI & Wellbeing Disclaimer

**Last updated: 2026-05-31**

Please read this before relying on Baynana.

## Baynana is a self-help tool, not therapy

Baynana uses an automated AI to help you reflect on your relationship. It is **not a
licensed therapist, counsellor, or medical professional**, and using it does **not**
create a professional relationship of any kind. Its responses are generated by software,
may be wrong, and should not be treated as professional advice.

For diagnosis, treatment, or personalised guidance, please consult a qualified
professional.

## Baynana is not for emergencies

If you are in immediate danger, experiencing abuse, or having thoughts of harming
yourself or someone else, **do not use this app for help**. Contact your local emergency
services or a crisis line right away:

- **Emergency services:** your local emergency number.
- If you are unsure where to turn, a hospital emergency department or a trusted person can
  help you find immediate support.

## The AI can make mistakes

AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or not right for your situation. You are
responsible for any decisions you make. Use your own judgment, and seek professional help
for serious or sensitive matters.

## On religious and traditional content

Baynana can include guidance drawn from Islamic tradition and from modern relationship
research, according to the preference you choose in settings. This content is offered for
reflection and is **not a religious ruling (fatwa)** or a substitute for guidance from a
qualified scholar.

## Your relationship is yours

Baynana offers perspectives to consider — it does not make decisions for you, take sides,
or tell you what to do about your relationship.
