# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-31**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Monther** ("we", "us") handles your
information in the **Baynana** mobile app ("the app"). Baynana is a private space
for couples to reflect on their relationship with the help of an AI guide.

We built Baynana to be **local-first and privacy-respecting**: much of your data
never leaves your device, and you can use the core experience without creating an
account.

By using Baynana, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use
the app.

---

## 1. The short version

- You can start using Baynana **anonymously** — no name, email, or account required.
- Your **profile, chat history, and daily check-ins are stored on your device**, not on our servers.
- To power the AI, the **text of your messages is sent to our AI providers** (OpenAI and Anthropic) to generate a reply. They process it to respond and do not use it to train their models under our agreements.
- If you **pair with a partner** or use **voice sessions**, some data is stored on our backend (Supabase) so the feature can work across two devices.
- You can **delete your account and all server-side data** at any time from the app.
- Baynana is **not a medical or therapy service** (see our AI Disclaimer).

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

- **Profile details** — the names you choose for you and your partner, and optional
  details you enter during onboarding or in your profile: gender, date of birth, relationship
  status, whether you have children, your general location (country and city, used so the AI
  matches your dialect and context), your relationship goals and challenges, and your
  preference for how religious/traditional wisdom appears in AI responses.
- **Your reflections** — the messages you send the AI, your daily check-ins, and your
  mood ratings.
- **Voice** — if you use a voice session, your speech is transcribed to text.

### 2.2 Information created as you use the app

- **AI memories** — to be helpful over time, the AI extracts short summarized
  "memories" from your conversations (for example, "prefers to be given space after
  an argument"). These are stored on our backend when you are paired or using
  cloud-backed features.
- **Pairing data** — if you connect with a partner, we store the link between your two
  accounts and any shared session content.
- **Session records** — weekly guided sessions, daily prompts, and voice transcripts
  tied to your account.

### 2.3 Account information

- If you use the app anonymously, we hold only an anonymous identifier.
- If you create or upgrade to a permanent account, we store the identifier from your
  chosen method — **email address** (email sign-in) or a token from **Apple** or
  **Google** sign-in. We do not receive your Apple/Google password.

### 2.4 What we do **not** collect

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not show third-party advertising.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 4. How the AI uses your information

When you send a message (or speak in a voice session), the text — along with relevant
AI memories and the profile details you provided (such as the names and ages you
entered, your relationship status, whether you have children, your general location,
and what you’ve said you’re working on) — is sent to our AI providers (**OpenAI**
and/or **Anthropic**) so the response can be tailored to your situation. This
processing is necessary to provide the core feature of the app.

Under our agreements with these providers, your content is used **only to generate
your response** and is **not used to train their models**. Each provider may retain
content briefly for abuse monitoring per their own policies.

---

## 5. The partner-sharing model

If you pair with a partner, you should understand how visibility works:

- Your **private solo conversations stay private** — your partner can never read them.
- The **AI remembers what each of you said privately**, so that in a **joint session**
  it can mediate with the full picture. It is instructed never to reveal one partner's
  private content to the other.
- **Couple-level insights** (recurring patterns, agreements, strengths) are visible to
  **both** partners.
- **Solo voice** stays private; **joint voice** is visible to both partners.

## 6. How we share information

We share data only with service providers that make the app work:

- **Supabase** — backend database, authentication, and serverless functions.
- **OpenAI** and **Anthropic** — AI response generation.
- **LiveKit** — real-time audio for voice sessions.
- **Apple** / **Google** — only if you choose those sign-in methods.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and
security of our users and the service. We do not sell personal data.

---

## 7. International data transfer

Our service providers may process and store data on servers outside your country,
including in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for
these transfers. By using the app you consent to this processing.

---

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **Use anonymously** — you can use the core app without giving identifying information.
- **Access & correct** — your profile and history are visible and editable in the app.
- **Delete** — *Profile → Delete account* permanently removes your account and all
  server-side data tied to it (AI memories, sessions, voice transcripts, pairing). This
  cannot be undone.
- **Sign out** — if you have a permanent account, signing out keeps your data so you can
  return.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as under the GDPR or
CCPA) to access, port, or object to processing of your data. To exercise these, contact
us at **monther_saigh@hotmail.com**.

---

## 9. Data retention

- Device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Server-side data remains until you delete your account, after which it is removed from
  our active systems. Backups are purged on a rolling basis.

---

## 10. Children

Baynana is intended for adults in relationships and is **not directed to children under
16**. We do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

---

## 11. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections, scoped database access rules
(row-level security), and server-held secrets for AI keys — to protect your data. No
system is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date
and, where appropriate, surfaced in the app.
